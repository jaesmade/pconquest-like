# Battle Lab

Battle Lab is a disposable 1v1 test battle reached from the title screen. It uses a 5×5 square arena with ally deployment in the bottom two rows, enemy deployment in the top two rows, and a neutral middle row. In zero-based grid coordinates, the ally starts at (2, 3) and the opponent at (2, 1). Allies initially face north and enemies face south. You command whichever Pokémon is active, including the opponent. Enemy AI does not act in this mode.

## Setup

Choose each Pokémon's species, level (1–100), and compatible held item, then choose starting weather and a positive numeric seed. The opponent also has a Normal (1×), Elite (2×), or Boss (3×) rank selector, defaulting to Normal. Rank multiplies HP, Attack, Defense, Special Attack, Special Defense, and Speed after level scaling, separate from temporary stages; Movement and AP gain remain unchanged. Both Pokémon begin at full HP. Each starts with its latest four distinct starting/level-up moves available at the chosen level, or fewer if its learnset is shorter. These are fresh test Pokémon; owned campaign Pokémon retain their chosen slots and manual level-up/TM choices.

## Inspecting results

- Select **Attack**, hover or focus a move to see its description, then choose it. Weather moves show a field forecast with the weather change, duration, and modifiers; Trick Room shows whether it will activate or end the current field. The board shows range and type effectiveness; click a valid tile to attack. Back returns to move choice without spending AP.
- The battle header shows weather and its remaining cycles; select the Weather field chip to review its effects and, for Sandstorm, the AV countdown to its next damage tick. Trick Room shows its remaining cycles beside it. Turn portraits show level, and the current/inspected Pokémon cards show level, HP, AP, action readiness, status, and timed stat stages with their rank, multiplier, and remaining cycles. Tile inspection shows coordinates, terrain, height, and active hazards.
- The **Damage lab** panel shows current HP and AP for both sides, starting seed, noncritical and critical damage ranges for the selected move, and the combat log. Damage ranges use the same pure calculator as the battle engine and do not consume RNG.
- **Replay seed** resets HP, statuses, items, weather, Trick Room, and the AV timeline. Equal-time turn ties and combat rolls repeat with the same choices and command sequence. Animation playback and unique visual event IDs do not affect combat RNG.
- The battle menu returns to setup. Battle Lab state is not written to the campaign save; the current run remains available from **Continue**.

The arena is deliberately plain and flat. Campaign maps remain the place to inspect terrain, elevation, and hazard interactions. The lab is for quick move, item, ability, animation, and damage comparisons.

## Scripted gameplay smoke playthrough

The playthrough also includes `scripts/ability-overhaul-checks.ts`: starting Capsule/Patch inventory, locked/unlocked slot eligibility, saved seeded offers, invalid/repeated consumption, every existing passive family in the hidden slot, combined damage feedback, ordinary evolution, Mega profiles, v28 ability/offer round trips, and active v27 battle continuity. Use the campaign route Bag to try both items in the rendered UI; fresh Battle Lab units still have their hidden slot locked.

The playthrough includes `scripts/ranked-encounters-checks.ts`: count boundaries through level 100; seeded previews and preparation re-entry; eight-enemy spawning for every template/arena across eight seeds; rank stats, Speed, paralysis/Trick Room, expiry and Mega recalculation; latest defaults for high-level recruits and short/duplicate learnsets; preserved manual XP/TM/evolution slots; and preparation/battle/intermission round trips plus v25/v26-to-v27 migration.

Run `npm run playthrough` locally to exercise a deterministic three-Pokémon draft, first route node, preparation, battle against the solo enemy planner, XP and coin rewards, and return to the route. The script checks that deployed participants gain encounter XP and a reserve does not, along with the 10,000-AV turn interval at reference Speed 65 and proportional rescheduling after a Speed increase, ±6 Speed-stage caps, stage expiry between cycles, Speed-stage interactions with paralysis and Trick Room, unchanged HP under stages, Trick Room's inversion around Speed 65 and field activation/expiry, fixed 3 AP gains with banking, one multi-tile Move costing 1 AP and blocked second Move, and one Attack per turn. It also uses Battle Lab state to check weather, stat stages, displacement, timed cover, Burn, and a Thunder Shock chain against a Ground-type secondary target. It checks that the enemy chooses useful weather and declines to recast it while active. Its player commands use a greedy damage-first policy and combine one Move with one Attack in the same turn when both are available. The script is a rules and progression check, not an assertion of optimal play or a rendered UI check. The latest result is recorded in [the project master record](PROJECT_MASTER.md).
